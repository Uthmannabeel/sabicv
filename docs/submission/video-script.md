# SabiCV — 3-minute demo video script

Recording plan: screen capture (phone-frame browser at sabicv.vercel.app +
admin screen + Selar dashboard) with voiceover. No talking head needed.
Target 2:45–3:00. One take per scene; stitch in any editor (CapCut works).

**Rule from past hackathons: the video is submitted, not perfect. Record it
the day before the deadline at the latest.**

---

### Scene 1 — The problem (0:00–0:25)
*Visual: a real Nigerian job advert (Jobberman/LinkedIn), then a generic CV.*

> "This is a real job in Lagos. And this is the CV of someone qualified for
> it who will never get the interview — because the CV doesn't say what the
> advert needs to hear. A professional rewrite costs ₦50,000 in Lagos.
> SabiCV does it for ₦3,500, in minutes, on your phone — and it's run by an
> AI agent, not a team of writers."

### Scene 2 — The free analysis (0:25–1:00)
*Visual: phone frame. Upload CV, paste job ad, submit. The score appears.*

> "Any job seeker starts free. The agent reads their actual CV against the
> actual advert and scores the match. No flattery — this candidate scored
> 20 out of 100, and the agent names exactly which gaps are costing them
> the job. That honesty is the storefront: you pay because the agent
> already proved it read your CV."

### Scene 3 — Payment and the agent at work (1:00–1:40)
*Visual: package selection → Selar checkout → order status page with the
agent journal ticking through steps live.*

> "Payment runs through Selar, a Nigerian merchant of record — so our
> revenue evidence is third-party records, not screenshots. The moment the
> sale is confirmed, the agent takes over: analysis, rewrite, and then the
> step that makes this a business and not a toy —"

### Scene 4 — The QA gate + truth receipt (1:40–2:20) ★ the signature scene
*Visual: agent journal showing qa step; then the truth receipt on the
delivered order — claims traced to source lines.*

> "— the agent audits itself. A second pass traces every claim in the new
> CV back to the customer's original. In our first live test it caught
> twenty-four fabricated claims in its own draft, rejected it, and forced a
> rewrite. The delivered CV ships with a truth receipt: every line traced
> to the customer's real experience, so they can defend every word in the
> interview. AI career tools that hallucinate get candidates blacklisted.
> Ours refuses to."

### Scene 5 — Delivery + the business (2:20–2:50)
*Visual: ATS-safe PDF opens; then quick cuts — admin dashboard with orders,
Selar sales export, agent execution log JSON.*

> "The customer gets ATS-safe PDFs minutes after paying. Behind it: one
> founder doing marketing and WhatsApp support, and Gemini doing every
> production decision — logged, per order, timestamped. [N] orders,
> [₦X] in revenue, [M] customers in [K] weeks. Marginal cost per order:
> a few API calls."

### Scene 6 — Close (2:50–3:00)
*Visual: landing page, URL on screen.*

> "SabiCV. Employment access, priced for the people who need it —
> operated by AI. sabicv.vercel.app."
