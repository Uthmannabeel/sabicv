Territory Sales Manager (FMCG) — Lagos

Reputable FMCG company seeks a Territory Sales Manager to drive secondary sales across Lagos mainland.

Key responsibilities:
- Achieve monthly sales targets and grow market share in the assigned territory
- Manage and expand a network of distributors and key retail accounts
- Negotiate trade terms, monitor sell-out, and manage credit exposure
- Lead and coach a team of 4 van sales representatives
- Prepare weekly and monthly performance reports for the Head of Sales using Excel and the company CRM
- Conduct market intelligence and competitor analysis

Requirements:
- Minimum 3 years field sales experience in FMCG or consumer goods
- Proven record of hitting sales targets and growing accounts
- Key account and distributor management experience
- Strong Excel and reporting skills; CRM experience is an advantage
- B.Sc in any discipline
- Strong negotiation, communication and leadership skills
